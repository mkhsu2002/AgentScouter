Like your result? Share your card!

The card contains only what you chose to put on it: name, species, optional birthday, level, and scores. It never contains your memory or conversation text.

Share card uses your device share sheet with the PNG when available, otherwise it copies the share text. Post on X opens a prefilled post. Post on Reddit opens a prefilled link submission; for a Reddit image post, download the card PNG and upload it there.

Privacy: this page has no backend and no tracking. Your input lives only in this browser tab and disappears when you close it. The only thing that ever leaves your device is the card image you download yourself.
